## Early recurring “why it went up” cues (from first extracts)

1. **Demand already proven** before the quiet period (prior leg up, not a bottoming guess).
2. **Supply dries up** in the rest (volume dry-up / low-volume pullback).
3. **Structure stays constructive** (MAs stacked or rising; shallow depth vs prior advance).
4. **Pivot is narrow / tight** so a small break implies committed buyers, not random noise.
5. **Expansion follows** with larger range and/or volume — the chart shows the payoff after the setup.

## Teacher quant fields (map to geometry)

- **Buying Force (BF):** quality of prior thrust — high BF (e.g. 53–58) co-occurs with strong impulse boxes.
- **Rest:** calendar length of pause (or dip days for pullbacks).
- **MA 10>20>50:** visual stack under price at trigger.
- **LF % in N days:** labeled post-entry payoff — training *outcome* text, not an entry cue.

## Anti-patterns to note when seen

- Wide, choppy bases with expanding volume into the high (supply still active).
- Pullbacks that slice through multiple MAs with heavy volume.
- Teacher note cases where setup conditions exist but entry was not taken (still valid structure training — e.g. Force Motors annotation).
