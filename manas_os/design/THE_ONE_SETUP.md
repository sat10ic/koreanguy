# The one setup — Pullback continuation

**One page. If it does not fit, we do not understand it.**
Frozen 2026-07-30. No threshold edits for 50 trades. Version: `PB-1`.

---

## What it is

A stock that has already proven it can run, resting on a shallow pullback, then
resuming. We are not calling a bottom and not chasing a breakout — we are joining a
move that is already working, at the one moment it offers a close stop.

## Universe (checked first, cheap)

- Price ≥ ₹30 · avg turnover ≥ ₹2cr/day (20d) · not an ETF
- Not ASM/GSM/T2T flagged · not circuit-locked on the trigger day

## The setup — all five must hold

1. **Leader** — RS ≥ 80, and in the top 20% of the market over 3–6 months
2. **Prior leg** — rose ≥ 25% from its 60-day low, on rising volume
3. **The rest** — pulled back **20–45%** of that leg, and is now **below its 10-day
   average** *(the only feature that separated winners from everything else across
   26 measures: winners retraced 35.6% vs 30.4%, and sat under the short MAs)*
4. **Tightening** — the last 5 bars' average range is smaller than the prior 20's,
   and volume over those 5 days is below the prior 20's
5. **Market permits it** — regime is RISK_ON or SELECTIVE, and breadth is not falling

If any one fails, there is no trade. No partial credit, no B grade.

## Entry

Buy on a move **above the previous day's high**, once conditions 1–5 hold.
No pivot buying. No gap chase — if it opens more than 2% above the trigger, skip it.

## Stop

**Below the low of the pullback, and never more than 4%.**
If a logical stop needs more than 4%, the setup is not good enough. Skip it.

## Size

`qty = capital × risk% / (entry − stop)`
Risk 1.0% per trade. Never average down. Never add to a loser.

## Exit

- **At +1R** — move the stop to breakeven and sell one third
- **Then trail** the 10-day average if the stock has historically held it
  (≥85% of closes above), otherwise trail the 21-day
- **Get out fully** when two of these fire within five sessions: closes below the
  trail · a high-volume down reversal · two distribution days · a lower low ·
  a gap-down rejection

## When NOT to trade

- Regime is DEFENSIVE or NO_TRADE
- Nothing on the shortlist followed through last week
- The last 3–5 trades broke out and came straight back — the tape is not paying
- More than 5 positions open, or open risk over the cap
- **Nothing qualifies. Showing zero names is the correct output most nights.**

## What would prove this wrong

After 50 trades: expectancy ≤ 0, or win rate below 25% with average win under 3×
the average loss. Then this page changes — not before.

## What we are measuring

win rate · avg win (R) · avg loss (R) · expectancy · holding days · MFE/MAE ·
how many of the market's ≥10% movers this setup caught (recall)
