# LENS: TradeTM Core (backbone — inject for EVERY debated name)

This is the doctrine backbone (`INDIA_PLAYBOOK.md` §1-6), not a setup-specific lens. Read it
before any family lens below. Source: `design/knowledge/INDIA_PLAYBOOK.md`.

## 1. Four-phase market model (read before judging any setup)
Classify the current tape into one of four phases, richer than bull/bear: **Demand Domination**
(bull bias, maximize frequency), **Supply Domination** (bear bias, fewer/higher-conviction bets),
**Lack of Demand** (buyers exhausted — most momentum-burst failures cluster here; favor
base-and-break / late-reaction EP variants over fresh gap-and-go), **Lack of Supply** (sellers
exhausted — long setups perform exceptionally well). [TTM-C1, TTM-C3, TTM-S20, TTM-S21, TTM-S24]
Regime is a **soft gate on setup mix, not a hard family kill** — D2/momentum can still work on
individual strength even in a muted tape. [TTM-C4]

## 2. RS-then-momentum: sequential, never conflated
RS is visible **during declines** (holds up best / falls least / green on a red day) — visual,
not a rating. Momentum is only confirmable **after** the stock has already moved. A momentum
stock almost always has RS; an RS stock does not always get momentum. Filter RS first, then
momentum, as two separate ordered stages — never blend them into one score. [TTM-C11, SG-High-RS]

## 3. Circuit constraints (hard, apply before any entry read)
- **>12% gap + first-5-min-ORB skip**: on an EP/gap day, if gap-up% + opening-range-high extension
  exceeds ~12% of prior close, skip — the 5% circuit removes the ability to get a same-day
  risk-free stop. [TTM-A1, TTM-S10]
- **5%-circuit-band exclusion**: any stock currently in the 5% circuit band is untradeable
  regardless of setup quality — risk is unmanageable. A circuit *upgrade* (5%→10/20%) is bullish;
  a *downgrade* to 5% means drop the name. [TTM-A2, TTM-S6]

## 4. Persistent vs Absolute momentum — opposite execution rules
The SAME chart behavior (a pullback to a moving average) is a **buy** under **persistent** (slow,
sustained trend — close >10EMA≥20d, >20EMA≥30d, >50EMA≥50d, >200EMA≥150d) rules, but a **stop-out**
under **absolute** (explosive, high-velocity, fresh-burst) rules. [TTM-H-III1] Applying a fixed-%
stop to a persistent name makes it "rotate around a moving average and hit your stop again and again."
Tag the name persistent-vs-absolute at entry and match execution:
- **Persistent** → buy pullbacks to the 20/50 EMA, NOT breakouts (a breakout entry on a persistent
  name often needs 15-20%+ upside to go risk-free; a pullback entry goes risk-free in 2-3%);
  wider structural stop, 50 DMA trail, pyramid on higher lows. [TTM-H-II1, TTM-H-III4, TTM-C10]
- **Absolute** → tight stop, LTF (lower-timeframe) trail, breakout/ORB entry is appropriate,
  quick exit into strength. [TTM-H-II1]

## 5. Trade-management template selection (by stock character, not pattern name)
Every setup gets ≤4 management rules under exactly one template — **setup-type MUST dictate
template**, and mismatches are a named failure mode:
- **Velocity** (momentum burst, D2, breakout, VCP, pullback-in-absolute-mode) → tight SL, trail
  on LTF MA, sell into strength / quick small gains. Mismatch: all-or-nothing on these → squats.
- **Magnitude** (EP, IPO base, high-conviction theme) → wider structural stop, sell into
  *weakness* (21EMA close-break, or 50DMA if tight), pyramid on pullbacks. Mismatch: managing
  these too tightly like a frequency trade → shakeouts.
- **Hybrid** → context-dependent blend of the above.
Read whether the stock is fast-and-spiky or slow-and-grinding (ADR/volatility tier proxy) to pick
the template — "you don't have only one way to buy a pullback." [TTM-F13, TTM-F16, TTM-H-IV2]

## 6. Governing line
**"Trade the market you are in, not the market you wish you were in."** [TTM-F15] — do not import
US frequency, universe, or stop-width assumptions into India's shallow, circuit-limited,
~500-liquid-name tape.
