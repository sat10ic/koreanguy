## THE EDGE-STACK SCORE (the unifying metric to build)
Per candidate, count INDEPENDENT edges present from the set above (0-6), render as STACKED CHIPS
+ one headline "N edges stacked". This is:
- the beginner-legible replacement for the pile of competing numbers (readiness/alpha/SMF/gates),
- exactly N.E.X.T's "one rating + evidence chips" (our simplification mandate),
- his "stack edges" made literal,
- a rank tiebreak candidate (more independent edges = higher conviction) — SHADOW/display first,
  gates only after replay proves stacked-edge count predicts forward return.
Rule: edges must be INDEPENDENT (don't double-count RS and sector-RS as two). Each chip cites its
source value. Honest absence: a missing edge shows greyed, not hidden — the beginner sees what it
LACKS, which is how he learns the combination.

## THE COMPOSED DAILY LOOP (15-min beginner ritual — the edge play)
1. **Regime gate** (Navigate): one mood number; his "EM>15" ≈ our XP band. Below threshold →
   SIT OUT is the edge. (Kills the choppy-tape false trades.)
2. **Leading industries** (Evaluate): industry-first; today's accelerating groups. Edge #4 lives here.
3. **Edge-stack scan** (eXecute): candidates ranked by edges-stacked; each card = N chips +
   deterministic plan (entry/stop/qty, one-writer). Anticipation-class setups (pre-trigger coils)
   get a WATCH card with armed pivot, not a refusal.
4. **Manage** (positions): coach per open trade — hold/trim/exit from the exit engine.
5. **Journal** learns: TAKEN/SKIPPED → expectancy per edge-stack-count × regime → the loop tells
   you WHICH stacks pay in YOUR hands.

The moat = steps 1+3+5 composed: refusal (regime+gate) × edge-stack conviction × private
expectancy. Indicators are commodities; this composition is not.
