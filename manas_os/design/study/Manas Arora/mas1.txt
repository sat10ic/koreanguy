Anmol is saying that if you hit four or five losing trades in a row, should you take open gains into consideration before you cut down your size on the upcoming trades? And the answer is agar aap open gain ko mind me rakh ke decide karenge kal agar market me crash aata hai open gain to jayega. Jo aapne nayi position li us gain ke basis pe uska bhi aapko loss bharna padega. To better hai ki aap sirf realized gain ke basis pe decide kare ki aapko slow down karna hai ya aggressive jana hai. Ek cheez aur kar sakte hai ki agar aapne stop loss ko trail kar liya hai. For example agar aapne 100 aapka original stop tha ab stock 110 rupay chal raha hai aur aapne trail karke 105 kar liya hai to us 105 tak aapne apne gain ko secure kar liya hai. Is portion ko aap count kar sakte hai. Decide karne ke liye ki me ko next trade me badi position leni hai ya size kam karna hai. To point ye hai ki jo gain aap secure kar chuke hai jo realize ho chuka hai wo zarur use kar sakte hai decide karne ke liye na ki open gains ko.
