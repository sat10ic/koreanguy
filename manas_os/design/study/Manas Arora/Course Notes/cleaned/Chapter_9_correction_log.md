## Book-build caution

This chapter is not merely "keep a journal." It is the performance-dashboard chapter.

The book version should preserve three layers:

1. **Behavioural layer:** journaling stops self-deception and catches recurring mistakes.
2. **Spreadsheet layer:** trade rows, split exits, original allocation, original risk, win/loss filters.
3. **System layer:** market breadth + historical trade outcomes tell the trader when to expect big moves and when to take smaller gains.

Do not compress it into a motivational paragraph about discipline. The spreadsheet mechanics are the lesson.
