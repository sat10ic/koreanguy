## Summary: Foundation 1

The first foundation is the **duration of the move** — for how many bars does the move likely continue? It's likely to continue 3 to 4 bars on average. By the way, it is true on the downside also — not just for the green bars. It is true for drops as well.

The first foundation is the duration of the move, which is **on average 3 to 4 bars**. If you already have so many green bars in a row, you know the stock is likely to take a pause or maybe drop. That's the first foundation.
