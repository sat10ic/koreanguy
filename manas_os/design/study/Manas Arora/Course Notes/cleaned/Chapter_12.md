Hi guys. The next chapter is: **How fast can you really grow your account?**

Though the title of this course is *Fast and Fortunate: How to Grow a Small Account Fast*, I get this message a lot, especially from new traders: “How much can we really expect?”

## Why hindsight is not the lesson

You must be wondering: this is so easy to do in hindsight. We can go back and do it after it has already happened. But how do you really find these moves in the real world?

The idea is: you do not know that the move you have right now is going to be a move like this. The only way you can really find a move like this is when you decide how to sell it.

The problem is that you are buying it and selling it every three or four days.

For example, if somebody bought somewhere here and then sold everything on this day, do you really think that in the entire rally from **₹200 to ₹3000** there was not even one day where the price action looked dangerous?

You will never go from ₹200 to ₹3000 without seeing a lot of extensions and a lot of dangerous-looking price bars like this.

If you are already rating your chart-reading skill so highly that you look at one day and decide, “This is dangerous, I am getting out,” and you are not letting the market decide your fate, then obviously you will never catch a move like this.

If you really want to catch a move like this, every trade you take has to be treated as if it could be the next great trade. You sit on it. Either you are stopped out with your rising 20-DMA, or if you see many obvious signs that it is extended, then you sell.

Looking at just one price bar and deciding that the game is over means you are trying to protect the gains of those two days.

If you are here to protect the gains of those two days, forget about catching a move like this. And then forget about doubling your money every other year.

## How to double every year — and who should not try

How do you double your money every year?

The answer is: you have to increase allocation. Increase allocation to maybe **30–35%**. Go on margin.

But again, I am warning you: this is not for somebody who is just trading. This is not for somebody who has not understood how markets work and does not have experience.

Going on margin and increasing size to 30–35% is for somebody who is experienced, has tasted good trades, and has the maturity to see gains evaporate.

For example, when you are here and the stock collapses, your paper gains are going down. If that is not affecting you, that is the person I am talking about. Such traders can push, increase allocation, and increase size.

With 20% allocation, if you are moving the account by 20–25% per trade, imagine what happens with 35% allocation.

If you catch five or six winners in a year, you can almost double your money that way.

## The wrong way to increase gains

The other way of increasing gains very fast is to find a lot of trades and play for small moves.

The problem with finding a lot of trades is that it is not suitable for 99% of us.

Finding a lot of trades and selling them every two or three days requires you to be on the screen all the time. You cannot really do anything else. You cannot do it part-time. You have to do it full-time.

Even if you are that one person who has the screen time, the burnout involved and the energy it takes will burn you out. Finding a lot of trades and selling them is not easy.

The peaceful way is this way: tell yourself that every trade you take can become one of these trades. Decide to sit for a few months.

Finally, there will be one trade that you actually catch.
