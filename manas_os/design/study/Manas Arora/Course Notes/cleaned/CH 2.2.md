## Why Magnitude Differs: Market Cap

Why? Because the duration — the first foundation — is the same on every frame, on every instrument, on every market, Indian or American or any third market. But the magnitude, the percentage, is not the same. We can always find a range, but every stock moves very differently.

The reason why we have different ranges is because of size: small caps, mid caps, and large caps.

If the stock is a small cap — which means only a few shares are available in the market, meaning very small supply is available — even a little demand can really explode the stock and it can move really fast. We have seen that, which is why Elecon had a range of 40–50% also. Paytm does not have that range at the lower end, because if you went to market cap, it is going to be definitely more than Elecon.

The point I'm trying to make here is that every stock has a different way of moving. We can judge it by looking at the past: if a stock has done this kind of a move before — if it is up 29% in four days — it tells you the nature of the stock. It can always repeat that in the coming future, which is why you can see 15% in three days. If I know this, from a tiny red day it went up almost 28% in eight days of similar behavior. Looking at the past, we can find out the range for that stock and take an average. We can never know for certain that it will go to 50% in three or four days, but we can get an idea.

## How to Use This Information

How will I use this information? It's very simple. If the stock is already up some 40% in the last three days, why would you go and take a trade after that fact? The likelihood of it going any further when it is up already 40% in no time — the likelihood of it offering you a great move is very low. That's how this foundation is important.

The duration of the move is the same, but the magnitude of the move will not be the same. Stocks move differently. Small caps will explode faster. Large names will not really move. You can see this: this is a four-day rally and it is only 5%, only 6%. We all are here to grow our account really fast, and if you really want to do that, you have to be invested only in stocks which have that potential. You can't really grow your account fast with Nifty and Reliance.

Understanding these foundations is very crucial, and again, in the coming chapters you will learn and you will realize how this information is going to help you on a day-to-day basis.

## Key Takeaways

That's your second foundation. The key takeaways here are:

- The average price movement is **3 to 5 bars** in either direction, on all time frames.
- No matter whether it is weekly, daily, or hourly — on any instrument, gold, bitcoin — you can verify this. In fact, I want you to verify this and believe it only when you have actually done this exercise.
- The **magnitude is typically 15 to 20%** (for stocks; I'm not talking about gold and cryptocurrencies — crypto is even faster than stocks). For stocks it's 15 to 20% on average, but the magnitude varies across different instruments and time frames.

You're seeing 41% in three days. This is a 16-minute frame of the same stock — if you see, this is 5 bars, 14%. The same stock on a smaller frame had the same duration (3 to 4 bars) but the magnitude changes. Just keep in mind: it's 15 to 20% on the daily for stocks.

Again, verify this study with the maximum number of stocks.
