## PART 1: COURSE OVERVIEW — "Fast and Fortunate"

**16-chapter course** (ASR-cleaned in `Course Notes/cleaned/`, curated in `cleaned/Manas/`). Foundation-first structure starting with psychology, not patterns.

### Chapter 1 — Fear Management (Foundation)
- Most traders obsess over setups/scans (easy); ignore fear management (hard). A scan tweet gets 750 likes; a drawdown video gets 50.
- **Two phases:** Learning Phase (first 30–50 trades — survival, not profit) and Thriving Phase.
- **Core line:** "Money is a by-product of following the process correctly."
- Real case: student lost ~5% year one, committed to non-interference, then hit two 20R trades and was up 20% in three months.

### Chapter 2 — Foundations & Cycles
- **Foundation 1 — Duration:** Moves last 2–7 bars (avg 3–4) on ALL timeframes, ALL instruments, ALL decades — verified on 1929 Dow Jones, Indian stocks 2008–2023, Bitcoin.
- **Foundation 2 — Magnitude:** ~15–20% per leg on daily. Small caps: 40–50%; large caps: 5–6%. Duration universal, magnitude varies by market cap.
- **Foundation 3 — Stage Analysis:** Stage 1 (basing) → Stage 2 (advance — ONLY stage to trade) → Stage 3 (topping/choppy) → Stage 4 (decline).
- **Base counting:** 1–2 bases = young/aggressive; 3+ bases = old/limited upside. Count resets when price breaks below lowest point of prior resting period.

### Chapter 3 — Layout, Indicators & Scans
- **Four-screen layout:** Weekly (10-week SMA, % from 52w low/high, distance from 10-week MA), Daily (10/20/50 DMA + purple dot + RVOL), Hourly/16-min (20-period MA), 15-min (micro entries).
- **Purple dot indicator:** Marks >5% move on >500K volume = institutional activity. Not entry/exit signal — visual screening convenience.
- **Four scans** (run after market close only):
  1. New 52-week high
  2. 100% up in 12 months
  3. Volume breakout (RVOL >3, up >3%)
  4. New one-month high (catches early turnarounds)
- Maintain a **focus list of 20–30 names** — ignore everything outside it. Stop at **5–7 names** for next day.

### Chapter 4 — Market Environment (Aggressive vs Defensive)
- **Most important skill:** Knowing when to be aggressive vs defensive. Trading one way all year is why people give back gains.
- **Three signs to go aggressive** (Feb–Mar 2023 case): (1) Monthly: 4 red months in a row (never had 5 in 23 years on CNX 500); (2) Daily: 3–4 bases forming, index refusing lower; (3) Breadth: extreme low (70–300 stocks above 20-DMA).
- **Aggressive approach:** Buy, don't sell early, add to winners, tolerate drawdowns.
- **Defensive signals:** 3+ bases old, 60°+ slope, breadth 1,300–1,500. Trail tighter, take partial profits, go to cash.
- **Key principle:** Don't wait for the obvious golden period — leaders show themselves during corrections. **Embrace corrections.**

### Chapter 5 — Two Setups: Continuation & Reversal
- **Continuation:** Strong uptrend → pullback to rising 20-DMA → tight consolidation (no red purple dots = no institutional selling) → entry. Three ingredients: (1) strong buying force (30–80% from 3-month low), (2) clean consolidation quality, (3) tight day.
- **Reversal:** Prior uptrend → multiple red days (3–5) → low volume on down days → buying force intact. Do NOT buy falling knife — only reversals within strong prior uptrend.
- **Weekly cross-check:** Mandatory. Reject if weekly extended (>20% from 10-week MA, 3+ green weeks in a row, 3+ bases old).
- **Big red day rule:** Ignore in consolidation if >10 days old OR its high has been crossed.

### Chapter 6 — Entry Techniques: Strong Start & Busted
- **Core philosophy:** Enter BEFORE the obvious breakout point (where crowd enters). Same risk, double reward.
- **Strong Start:** Gap-up above previous close; low does NOT breach previous close; buy above previous day's high after 2–3 minutes. RVOL in first minutes is bonus. Stop: ~4% (beginners), tighten to day's low (~2.5%) after 20–30 trades.
- **Busted (15-min reversal):** Place alert at key support level. When breached, place second alert above breach bar's high. Must trigger within 2–3 bars (30–45 min max) — urgency critical. If takes longer, quality is poor.
- **Pullback:** Controlled dip in uptrend. Multiple entries possible (Zentech example: 4 entries).

### Chapter 7 — Position Sizing
- Formula: Account size × risk % ÷ stop distance = number of shares.
- **Risk % ≠ investment %.** 0.5% risk with 2% stop = ~20% account invested but only 0.5% at risk.
- **Learning phase:** 0.1–0.2% risk per trade. **Running phase:** 0.3–0.5%.
- **Gap-down fear solved by math:** 10% gap-down on 0.5% risk trade = 2.5% account loss. 20% gap-down = 5% loss. Recoverable, not account-ending.
- **Mistake sheet:** Write every mistake daily. Review reduction over time.

### Chapter 8 — Ride or Sell (Trade Management)
- **Young market + young move:** Do NOT interfere. Move stop to breakeven after first strong day. Trail with 20-DMA on **closing basis** (not intraday). Emergency stop below latest swing low.
- **Old/extended market:** Sell into strength. Take 50–75% partial profits. Trail remaining with 10-DMA or 5-DMA.
- **Extension warnings:** 3–4 bases old, 50%+ from base, angle ~90°, many green days in a row, high volume near top, second extension after short rest.
- **Emergency stop:** Give stock 2–3 bars to react at key MA; minor closes below MA acceptable when move is young.

### Chapter 9 — Trading Journal
- **"What you measure gets monitored."** Columns: stock, qty, entry/exit, stop, holding period, return %, P&L, allocation %, risk %.
- Sample metrics (18 trades): 61% win rate, avg winner 3.82%, avg loser 0.71%, avg allocation 16.25%, avg risk 0.27%.
- Cross-reference winning trades with market breadth to identify conditions producing 10% moves vs 3–5% moves.

### Chapter 10 — Understanding Your Edge
- **Edge is NOT the setup.** Finding stocks above 20-DMA is learnable in 1–2 months.
- **Real edge is behavioral:** (1) Waiting for right conditions, (2) What you do between two great trades, (3) Riding in good markets, cutting in bad, (4) Execution under fear.
- "If your mistake sheet isn't nearly blank after 3 months, reconsider trading."

### Chapter 11 — Drawdowns
- **Down 3% from peak → halve size. Down 5% → stop entirely, disconnect, close everything.**
- Don't change execution — change only risk size. Stock doesn't know your P&L.
- **Recovery:** Tiny positions. Let 1–2 work. Increase only if traction returns. Don't watch balance daily.

### Chapter 12 — How Fast Can You Grow?
- Historical big movers: HEG (₹200→₹3000, 11 months), Mirza (6× in 10 months), Mazdock (3× in 3 months), CDSL (₹290→₹1,500 in 11 months). Average big move duration: ~5–6 months.
- **Portfolio impact:** 20–25% allocation on 100% stock move = 20–25% portfolio return from one trade.
- Doubling every 1–2 years realistic with discipline. **"Treat every trade as a potential big winner — sit on it until trailing stop hits."**

### Chapter 13 — Market Breadth
- Tracks: stocks up/down >4.5%, up 20% in 5 days, above/below 20/50 DMA.
- **Only useful at extremes:** 1,300–1,500 above 20-DMA = tops; 50–300 = bottoms.
- At 1,400: shift from 5th gear to 3rd gear (trail tighter, partial exits). At 200–300: go aggressive.

### Chapter 14 — Outperforming Sectors
- Weekly process: MarketSmith → sort 197 groups by market cap → find improving rank (3-month) → check group chart → pick RS stocks making higher lows while index makes lower lows.
- Stop at 5–7 names from strong groups — enough for the week.

### Chapter 15 — Daily Routine
- Evening: scan → build 5–7 name watchlist. Morning: execute Strong Start / Busted → **stay away from live action**.
- Weekend: update strong-group list, review journal, update trailing stops.
- **Progressive overload:** Increase account size gradually (₹1L → ₹5L → ₹10L → ₹20L). Always use percentages, not rupee terms.

### Chapter 16 — Live Watchlist Construction
- Applied walkthrough. Key rejections: low force/no purple dots, choppy history, 3rd base after short rest, climax acceleration (22°→77° MA angle in days).
- Selections: large base breakout with 70% force, fresh weekly, 50-DMA first touch after 6–7 red days = 5-star Busted.
- **Core lesson:** "If you want the full move, buy from an ugly-looking setup."

### Strong Start Tightness Study (Supplemental)
- **>80% of successful Strong Starts** had an extremely tight day immediately before entry (volatility contraction → expansion).
- No fixed threshold — compare visually to stock's own history.
- **Exception:** After 5 consecutive down days, a different setup (reversal) applies.
- **Warning:** 4th green day in row is NOT valid Strong Start even if it works.

### Groww Series (groww 1–10, Hindi Video Transcripts)

**groww 1 — What is Swing Trading:** Three trading styles: Intraday (stressful, screen-bound), Long-term (safe but slow), Swing (middle ground — 11-day 36% move, 25-day 43%, 68-day 85%). Swing = catch one leg of zigzag, avoid corrections.

**groww 2 — Stock Selection Checklist:** 5,000 stocks → need 10. Three-month performance >30% (buying force). Purple dot indicator (>5% on >500K volume = fast-moving stock). Avoid low-liquidity stocks. Maruti example: zero purple dots in its entire move = slow mover.

**groww 3 — Scanning:** One scan is enough: **volume-based scan**. All big moves start with massive volume increase. NSE only (BSE has 1/25th liquidity of NSE for same stock). Filters: price >30, day change >3%, avg volume >100K.

**groww 4 — VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern):** Not just a pattern — a **principle of market operation**. Selling pressure reduces with each correction (100→70→80→90→95). Tightness shows sellers losing ground; breakout follows when buyers overwhelm.

**groww 5 — Summary Video:** Small cap swings: 151% up, then 64% next swing. Large cap swings: 20–30% only. Know your candidate's nature from past moves. Minimum criteria recap: 30%+ from 3-month low, purple dots, correct market cap for your style.

**groww 6 — Strong Start Entry (Early Entry Before Textbook):** Don't wait for textbook VCP breakout (break of latest contraction's high). Textbook entry at ₹7,550 on Jen Eco; Strong Start entry far earlier at gap-up above previous close + RVOL confirmation. **Buy inside the base, not at the top.**

**groww 7 — Trade Management / Selling:** "Taking a loss is the easiest part." Real difficulty begins when trade goes in profit. **Simple rule for beginners:** Sell when it hits your stop OR when it gets super-extended. This solves 99% of selling problems.

**groww 8 — Full Case Study (Indian Renewable Energy):** Scanned Dec 12, tracked for 1 month, selected Jan 11. Massive uptrend + clean consolidation (no big red days) + purple dots + right side of cup forming. Patience is key — don't run new scans daily and chase new names.

**groww 9 — Mindset:** Technicals are 10% of trading. Rest is mindset. Year 1 goal: **survive**. Every mistake costs money — compare to learning music where mistakes are free. Accept tuition fee. First year: learn the skill. Profit comes later.

**groww 10 — Market Environment:** Even perfect setups fail in wrong environment. Laxmi Organic example: perfect Strong Start entry (gap-up, mini shakeout, cross high) → 18% in 2 days. But market was weak (NFT Midcap below 20-DMA) — had to manage carefully.

**ma9 — Environment Analysis:** "When you combine stocks with right environment, you make easy money. Wrong environment = hit rate tanks." Live breadth analysis — 1,400 green stocks = overheated.

**ma11 — Q&A: Gap-Down, Volume, Weekly MAs:** Volume confirmation in first 10 minutes (3–4 min with 10–20% of avg volume = high volume day). Stop discipline: no second-guessing.

**ma13 — Q&A: Gap-Down Position Management:** Starting with 10% position. Regardless of size (10/20/50/90%), if stop is hit, you exit.

**ma19 — Purple Dot Volume Marker:** Custom indicator. 1M+ volume moves marked. Not entry/exit signal — just screening convenience.

**ma20 — How to Study Bases:** Move focus from breakout point to entire base formation. Multiple purple dots inside base = institutional accumulation = higher probability. "1M volume happening again and again is not retailers' volume — it is definitely a big institution."

**ma21 — Scanner Techniques:** Report Designer → 3-month performance → sort top 30–50. **"Whatever rule you follow, you will miss something. Form one rule and be okay with it."**

**mas1 — Realized vs Open Gains:** Make sizing decisions based on **realized gains**, not open gains. Exception: if stop-loss has been trailed (secured).

**mas2 — Events (Dividends, Splits):** Dividends: can hold (price drops ≈ amount, recovers). Stock split: DO NOT hold — new shares take 10–15 days to credit; cannot sell during that window.

**mas3 — Psychological Baggage:** Old loss baggage causes premature profit booking. **Drop old baggage. Trade with a clean slate.** Book some profits for confidence if needed, then move past loss-recovery mentality.

**mas4 — Regret Management:** "This business is full of regrets." Stand by your decisions once taken. Learn to distinguish market conditions (30–50% swing markets vs 10% difficult markets).

**mas5 — Screen Obsession in Swing Trading:** Solution: cut position size to less than half. Take next 5 trades purely to control the urge. Systematic desensitization.

**mas6 — Handle Stop-Outs & Re-Entries:** Put stopped-out names in separate watchlist. If they set up again, give them **priority over new names** — weak hands flushed, next move cleaner. Often buy **larger** next time (not revenge — confidence that weak hands are gone).

## PART 3: Q&A FILES — KEY CLARIFICATIONS

**qna1 — Old vs Young Moves:** If stock is up 60%+ from base with minimal rest = old. Corrections of 15–20%+ are necessary to flush weak hands.

**qna1.2 — Watchlist Protocol:** Start with watchlist → practice Strong Start/Busted with 0.1% risk + 4% stop. For first 50 trades, check EVERY criterion. Overtrading warning: 6 trades in 2 days = too many.

**qna 1.3 — Pyramiding & Problematic Bars:** Pyramiding = buying more as stock goes up. A big red bar on high volume can be ignored if its high gets crossed later (trapped weak hands exit).

**qna 1.4 — First 20–30 Trades:** Do NOT interfere on day-to-day basis. Only two exits: trailing stop hit or super-extension. **First entry = largest (10–15%); subsequent additions = smaller (4–7%); goal = 25% total allocation.**

**qna 1.5 — Flushing / Correction Concept:** 20–25% drop after uptrend resets the base — weak hands flushed. Approached as "young" again. Strong Start replaces conventional breakout.

## MASTER SUMMARY: TOP 20 CORE PRINCIPLES

1. **Process over money.** Money is a by-product of correct process. Interfering with trades out of fear/greed is #1 return destroyer.

2. **Market environment determines everything.** 3/4 stocks work in good conditions; 3/4 fail in bad. Aggressive when breadth low + market young; defensive at 1,300+ breadth + 3+ bases old.

3. **Moves last 3–4 bars on average** across all timeframes, instruments, and eras. After 3–4 bars in one direction, pause or reversal likely.

4. **Only trade Stage 2 stocks** (advancing with rising MAs). Base count: 1–2 bases = aggressive; 3+ = limited upside.

5. **Two setups: Continuation & Reversal.** Both require strong buying force (30%+ from 3-month low), clean consolidation (no high-volume red days), and tight action.

6. **Enter BEFORE the breakout.** Strong Start (gap-up holding previous close) and Busted (15-min reversal with urgency within 2–3 bars). Same risk, double reward.

7. **Position sizing solves everything.** Risk % of account, not investment %. 0.5% risk per trade. Gap-down fear is solved by math.

8. **Young market → ride, don't interfere.** Trail with 20-DMA on closing basis + emergency stop below swing low. Move to breakeven after strong day 1.

9. **Old/extended market → sell into strength.** Take 50–75% partial profits. Trail remaining with 10-DMA or 5-DMA.

10. **Your edge is behavioral, not technical.** The setup is easy. The edge is: waiting, riding, cutting, executing under fear.

11. **Drawdown control is non-negotiable.** Down 3% → halve size. Down 5% → stop entirely. Reduce size, not execution quality.

12. **Big money comes from sitting on trades for months.** Historical winners moved 2–10× over 3–11 months. 20–25% allocation = 20–25% portfolio return.

13. **Use breadth only at extremes** (1,300+ or 50–300 stocks above 20-DMA). Not daily. Combine with index analysis.

14. **Weekly sector rotation scan:** improving group rank → group chart → RS stocks making higher lows while index makes lower lows.

15. **>80% of successful Strong Starts** had extreme prior-day tightness. Volatility contraction precedes expansion.

16. **Purple dots inside base = institutional accumulation** = higher probability. Skip stocks with no institutional interest.

17. **Track stopped-out names for re-entry** — weak hands flushed, next move cleaner. Often buy larger the second time.

18. **Money comes in clusters.** 2–3 months produce bulk of annual returns. Preserve capital the rest of the year.

19. **"You create alpha by not losing much in bad markets."** Hit rate ~30% is fine when losers are tiny and winners ride.

20. **One year / 100 deliberate trades** to go from newbie to competent. Journal everything. Read Trading in the Zone every 6 months.
