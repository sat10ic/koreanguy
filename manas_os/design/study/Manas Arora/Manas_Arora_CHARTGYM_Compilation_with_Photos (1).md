# Manas Arora (@iManasArora) — #CHARTGYM Exercises Compilation
**With Accurate Photo Captures from "How I'd Trade" Threads**

**Series Purpose**  
#CHARTGYM is Manas Arora’s weekly interactive educational series. Every weekend he posts a **blank historical stock chart** and asks followers to annotate it as if they were trading it live: mark entry, exit, stop-loss, and note their thoughts at each stage.  

The goal is deliberate practice — building chart-reading muscles, trade management intuition, and the ability to handle real-market surprises. “Just like the gym, the more reps you put in here, the more effortless trading becomes out there.”

This compilation includes:
- Full verbatim exercise posts (blank chart + instructions)
- **Accurate photo dumps** of the “This is how I’d trade” multi-image explainer threads (with direct image links + detailed visual descriptions captured from the actual photos)
- Links to every original post and image

**How to Use**  
1. Read the exercise post.  
2. Study the blank chart description.  
3. Pause and mentally (or on paper) annotate your own plan.  
4. Then open the “How I’d trade” photos and compare with the author’s step-by-step thinking.  
5. Repeat across different market regimes for maximum skill transfer.
